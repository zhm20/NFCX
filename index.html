The NFC tasks you use, without a pile of terminal windows.

NFCX focuses first on MIFARE Classic 1K and the PN532 UART reader path. It keeps the reader, keys, dump workspace, and verification in one desktop application.

Reader & card discovery

Find supported readers, connect with the exact connection string, and view card type, UID, ATQA, and SAK.

Read, edit & restore

Work with standard raw dumps, inspect sectors and blocks, and verify every written block by reading it back.

Run authorized common-key, Darkside, Nested, and Hardnested workflows with cancellable progress and logs.
